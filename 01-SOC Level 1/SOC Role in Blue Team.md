# SOC Role in Blue Team

## Introduction

This room introduce the SOC role in an organization or in an enterprise structure. 

## Security Hierarchy 

Typical security organizations include: Executives, Security Leaderships, Security Managers, and Technical level employer.

- Executives - Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Security Leaderships - Chief Technological Officer (CTO), CIO (need long form), and Chief Information Security Officer   (CISO)
- Security Managers - Security Managers, Team Lead
- Technical - Analyst, Engineer, Red Teamer

### Security Departments

Not all companies have a "security departments". For small companies, the IT departments takes the role of securing the company. But for those who do have security departments, they are divided to this:

- Red Team - includes pen-testers and ethical hackers and other offensive security experts 
- GRC Team - includes specialists managing security policies, and ensuring compliance with regulations like PCI_DSS
- Blue Team - includes SOC analysts, security engineer, incident responders and other defensive security experts

## Meet the Blue Team

Blue Team is about defensive security and the people on the blue team monitor attacks constantly, and they try to respond them quickly. And here are the most common Blue Team departments:

### Security Operations Center (SOC)

An SOC department is composed of following roles:

- L1 analysts
- L2 analysts
- Engineers 
- Managers

### Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT)

These are like "firefighters". If the SOC team couldn't handle and incident goes out of control, you call them. They are also called CSIRT or CERT. Here are the famous examples of CIRT:

- JPCERT
- Mandiant
- AWS CIRT

### Specialized Defensive Roles

Roles supporting the Blue Team:

- DevSecOps
- Penetration Tester
- GRC auditor

Some large companies have some specialized and narrow blue team roles that requires deep topic knowledge and broad experience in SOC or IT fields. They include:

- Digital Forensics Analyst
- Threat Intelligence Analyst
- AppSec Engineer
- AI Researcher

## Internal SOC vs MSSP

MSSP means Managed Security Services Provider. Sometimes companies who don't usually have their own SOC team, hire them to respond incidents. Working at MSSP is typically high-pressure, but it is also a good option to quickstart your career.
